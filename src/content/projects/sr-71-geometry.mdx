## Surface geometry
The uploaded archive contains a textured GLB and an STL surface model of the SR-71. The interactive viewer makes the aircraft’s overall form, surface structure, and cross-sections accessible directly in the browser.

## Scope
This entry is a geometry visualization. The archive does not include solver settings, flow results, or an original aircraft-design claim. Model authorship and provenance are still being documented.
