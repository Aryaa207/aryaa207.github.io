## Contribution and objective
I prepared geometry and generated a computational mesh for a Mach 5 external-flow study in ANSYS Fluent. The analysis examined shock structure, pressure distribution, and the interpretation of aerodynamic heating within the chosen model.

## Computational setup
The documented setup uses 3,389,998 tetrahedral cells, a density-based implicit solver, SST k–ω turbulence, ideal-gas air, and Sutherland viscosity. Minimum orthogonal quality is 0.2006. The mesh count and type come from the Fluent report dated 15 February 2026.

## Flow-field evidence
The original Mach and pressure contour exports show the saved flow fields, with their legends retained. A workstation photograph also shows an early drag-monitor history. These provide a record of the analysis setup and visualization work.

## Validation boundary
The archived report records zero iterations in its solution-status section, and the saved coefficient differs from an earlier portfolio value. A final converged drag coefficient cannot be established from those records. This case study therefore presents the setup and contour evidence without treating either coefficient as validated.

## Model limits
Ideal-gas assumptions limit the interpretation of high-temperature flow. A quantitative heating claim would require an appropriate thermodynamic model and validation evidence. A numerical DATCOM comparison is not included in the current record.
