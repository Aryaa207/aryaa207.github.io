## The problem
Develop a free-flight glider while connecting aerodynamic estimates to a manufacturable design.

## My contribution
As project manager on a team of four, I led airfoil selection, Reynolds-number estimation, and glide-ratio prediction. I created the CAD model and technical drawings in NX Siemens and coordinated fabrication and flight testing.

## Engineering method
The aerodynamic work connected airfoil selection and operating Reynolds number to expected glide performance. CAD and technical drawings provided the basis for fabrication and subsequent flight evaluation.

## The outcome
The team completed a CAD-to-flight-test workflow. The source material does not yet include numerical glide-ratio predictions, flight measurements, or comparison plots.
